# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Next.js with React and TypeScript for the web product, matching the supplied architecture. The production service boundary is a Java 21 Spring Boot modular monolith backed by PostgreSQL and Redis; this initial repository implements the web experience foundation.

## Users

Developers, builders, students, founders, and mentors, launching India-first and designed to expand globally. They want to meet credible peers, make a useful first connection quickly, then retain enough shared context to collaborate later.

## Product Purpose

CONFLUX.site is a verified, people-first networking product for developers. It takes someone from wanting to meet an interesting builder to a verified, mutual connection through a low-friction, one-to-one 60-second conversation.

## Positioning

It makes spontaneous developer networking intentional without becoming a social feed: verified people meet briefly, choose Connect or Next, and can progress into a durable relationship and Build Room.

## Operating Context

The core journey is landing, sign-up and verification, compact profile and intent setup, Meet queue, time-bounded conversation, mutual decision, chat and revisit. Finite discovery surfaces a small relevant set of people and active builds. Pro makes deeper outcomes possible through targeted discovery, direct requests, extended sessions, screen sharing, Build Rooms, relationship memory, and AI-assisted next steps.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Free users can Meet, Connect, chat, revisit connections, and voluntarily share normal contact details or external links. These are never artificially paywalled.
- Trust is mandatory: email, LinkedIn, and GitHub verification are product signals; verification status must be accurate and server-derived.
- Discovery is finite and deliberate, never an infinite social feed.
- Text is first-class. Voice, video, and screen sharing are opt-in and recoverable.
- V1 prioritizes one-to-one networking; groups, organizations, and event modes are later work.
- The full architecture calls for server-authoritative authorization, entitlements, session state, matching, privacy, safety, billing webhooks, and realtime events.

## Brand Commitments

Use the CONFLUX.site name and product vocabulary: “Meet someone”, “Connect”, “Next”, and “Build together.” The experience should be confidence-building, human, contemporary, and Gen Z-aware without becoming gamified or noisy.

## Evidence on Hand

The authoritative product material is in the supplied architecture report and phase-wise plan, including their v2 addenda. No production testimonials, customer claims, photography, or brand asset pack was provided.

## Product Principles

1. Human connection is the product; intelligence supports context and discovery rather than replacing conversation.
2. Trust and user control are prerequisites to participation.
3. Make a meaningful first meeting faster than a traditional networking workflow.
4. Free creates the network; Pro creates intentional access and deeper collaboration without punishing a successful free connection.
5. Every valuable connection should be easy to recover, understand, and move forward.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The product must work on mobile and desktop, with keyboard navigation, visible focus, clear labels, motion sensitivity support, and text-first paths when media permissions or connectivity fail.
