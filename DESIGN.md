# CONFLUX Visual System

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Direction

CONFLUX uses an **after-hours build lab** visual world: the useful, expressive atmosphere of a late developer meetup where sticky notes, live room boards, and credible proof-of-work coexist. It is an Operate interface first—dense when a user is in a live Meet, calmer when they are deciding what to do next.

This is the assigned direction from the concept round. It is raised by the discipline of dance notation: a visible central timeline gives time-bound conversations a legible rhythm without borrowing its aesthetic.

## Product expression

- **Meet** is the focal interaction: a large, two-sided presence panel with a living 60-second progress rail, concise shared context, and decisive Connect / Next actions.
- **Trust is small but visible:** compact verification marks and GitHub proof replace heavy identity theatre.
- **Discovery is a finite dispatch:** a bounded board of people worth knowing, each with a human-readable match reason; it never behaves like a feed.
- **Build Rooms are workspaces:** goals, notes, relationship memory, and a clear next action share one practical surface.

## Tokens

- Ink: `#111318`; bone: `#F4F1E8`; cobalt: `#3157D5`; lime: `#C8FF45`; coral: `#FF7359`; muted ink: `#555B66`; line: `#D7D2C6`.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque for interface and DM Mono only for clocks, match counts, and compact measurements.
- Corners are 14px; controls are deliberately squared-off within that language. Depth uses one soft offset shadow, not glow.
- Cobalt signals navigation and live presence, lime signals readiness/connection, coral signals safety or time-sensitive action.

## Behavior

- Motion is restrained: the live rail advances, queue status pulses once, and side panels enter by clipping from their source edge. Reduced motion disables these flourishes.
- Keyboard focus is an explicit cobalt outline; selection and scrollbar colors belong to the system.
- Mobile reorganizes the live comparison vertically and keeps the primary decision visible without requiring hover.

## Direction contract

The product must feel more like arriving at a credible builder room than entering a social app. It may be energetic and contemporary, but never gamifies activity or turns a time-bound meeting into an arcade.
